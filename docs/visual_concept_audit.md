# Visual concept audit

The file `dashboard/mexico_toys_dashboard_mockup_v2.png` is an early visual direction, not a screenshot of the completed Tableau workbook.

## Validated values

The following headline values agree with the reproducible project exports:

- Revenue: **$14.4M**
- Units sold: **1.09M**
- Gross profit: **$4.0M**
- Gross margin: **27.8%**
- Jan–Sep revenue growth: **30.9% YoY**
- Jan–Sep gross margin: **29.6% in 2022 to 26.2% in 2023**
- Airport gross profit per store: approximately **$126K**
- Saturday average daily revenue: approximately **$31.1K**
- Stockouts with recent demand: **77**
- High-cover or no-recent-sales inventory at cost: **$150.4K**

## Illustrative elements

- The monthly revenue and gross-margin curves in the mockup were drawn for visual exploration. Their plotted heights and shapes do not reproduce the monthly export and must not be interpreted as measured values.
- The Magic Sand / Colorbuds panel places a revenue change and a gross-profit change on one diverging axis. Because these are different measures, the panel is not a valid like-for-like comparison.

## Authoritative deliverable

The authoritative interactive deliverable is `dashboard/mexico_toys_tableau.twb`. Its worksheets use the project exports and should be used for analysis, screenshots and portfolio evidence. The concept may still guide a future visual polish pass, provided the Tableau marks and annotations remain tied to validated metrics.
